Privacy Policy

Your documents, your phone number and your cargo details are personal. We treat them that way. This page explains in plain words what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do. Effective 6 November 2026.

Who we are

Tee Tuu Ltd is a Certified Customs Licensed Agent in Lagos, Nigeria. We do clearing and forwarding, and we deal in automobile sales. Contact us at teetuu31@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +234 905 269 1392.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Shipment and vehicle details: what you are importing or buying, where it comes from, and the port it arrives at.
  • Documents needed for customs clearing, such as invoices, bills of lading and identification, when you choose to send them.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by phone, by email or on Facebook.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and give you a quote.
  • To prepare customs declarations, clear your cargo and arrange forwarding or delivery.
  • To sell you a vehicle and complete the paperwork.
  • To keep the records the law requires us to keep.

Our website

This site has no accounts, no ads and no analytics trackers. The contact form does not store or send anything to us. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and WhatsApp (Meta) then handles that message under its own terms. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when the page loads.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to do your job: the Nigeria Customs Service and other regulators, shipping lines, terminal operators, transporters, and your bank or insurer when relevant. We never sell your data.

Your rights

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or stop using it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email us and we will respond. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet legal and record-keeping duties. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Keeping it safe

We limit who can see your documents and use reasonable measures to protect them. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, so please share only what is needed.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
